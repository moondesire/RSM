How to Get a Webhook URL
  1. Open Discord and go to the channel you want notifications in.
  2. Click the gear icon next to the channel → Integrations → Webhooks.
  3. Click New Webhook, name it NBA Portal, then click Copy Webhook URL.
  4. Paste the URL above and click Save Webhook.
  5. Click Send Test Message to confirm it works.
